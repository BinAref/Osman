Community Center

Form found in paper

The form of this community center was found by hand, in paper, before it was drawn and modelled.

Drawings
Site plan 1:500; floor plans, sections and elevations

  1. 1I started with visual research, studying many buildings with complex shapes.

  2. 2Then I cut paper and layered the sheets to build a complex shape with an artistic character.

  3. 3I glued the papers and shaped the corners and edges of the building.

  4. 4I added the external parts while choosing the materials.

  5. 5In the end, the final shape of the building.
